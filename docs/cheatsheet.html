The basics

SELECT
turns the pages of EDIT, LFO, ENV, FX, GLO · elsewhere: the row / step page / menu
ALGORITHM
the track · SONG: the entry · GROOVE: the step · OPTIONS: the metronome
PRESETS
SOUND 1: the factory sound · elsewhere: the page's cursor or the pattern
K1–K4
red, yellow, green, blue · the bottom line always says what they do
a button again
the next page of its group (EDIT, LFO, ENV, SCL, GLO, FX)
OCT− OCT+
the keys' octave · other pages: size, add/remove, OK/back

Holds a yellow bar fills first

HOMEHOLD
the help of the page you are on
HOMEHOLD+PLAY
PANIC: everything stops and cuts
FXHOLD
LIVE effects, for as long as you hold it
SAVEHOLD
the tools: OPTIONS, UNDO, REDO, CLEAR, COPY, PASTE, RANDOM
SAVEHOLD+OCT−/OCT+
UNDO / REDO, at once
RECHOLD+SELECT
rows the cursor jumps after a note (0–16)

Shortcuts

SAVE OCT+
save the song again, in its slot
OCT−/OCT+ (GRID)
the pattern's size: 16, 32, 64 steps
OCT+/OCT− (a sound in gold)
load the factory sound shown / keep yours
ARPHOLD+PRESETS
SOUND: next / previous engine
ARPHOLD+K3/K4
the cell's second effect
ARP tap
clear what is under the cursor

The ARP key clear / shift, not an arpeggiator

TRACKS
tap: clear cell · held+SELECT/ALGO: insert / remove a row
GRID
a step held + ARP: clears it
SOUND
held + PRESETS: next / previous engine
SONG
switches LOOP ⇄ SONG
LIVE
clears the LIVE lane
MIX
held + a black key: solo that track

Keys white / black, per page

GRID
16 steps on / off · black: the track
MAGIC
the 16 tools · black: the track
LIVE
the bank's effects · black: mute tracks
MIX
play the track · black: mute · ARP held: solo
SONG / GROOVE / PROJECTS
the entry's pattern (or “+”) / the step / the slot
KIT, sliced SAMPLE
black keys play too: the neighbour, an octave up

Sound engines

PULSE
12/25/50/75% or PWM, NES-style
WAVE
8 Game Boy-style 4-bit waves
NOISE
LONG or METAL, tuned by the note
FM
6 operators, 32 algorithms · one track at a time
KIT
16 drums, 8 kits
SAMPLE
a break in 16 slices, or your own sound
