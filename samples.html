Put your own sounds into your FM-1

Drop WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC files here, choose a slot (MY 1 to MY 4), and send them to your FM-1 over USB. The page makes them what KAWA stores: mono, 22 kHz, 8 bits, up to 1.66 seconds. Nothing leaves your computer.

This page needs install/websamples.js next to it (it did not load). Open the page from the repository or the website, not on its own.

BEFORE YOU SEND

  • KAWA 0.2 or later must be running on the FM-1 (the tracker screen), plugged into this computer with a USB data cable, in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  • KAWA must be stopped: press PLAY so nothing is playing, and leave the FM-1 alone until the transfer ends. The FM-1 is silent while a slot is being written.
  • A slot that is sent to is replaced. If the cable is pulled or anything goes wrong, that slot stays empty: never half written. Send it again.

      What happens to a sound, and how to use it
      • It is mixed to mono and brought to 22 050 Hz (or 11 025 Hz if you choose Longer: twice as long, duller). WAV files are read by this page, exactly as install/kawa_samples.py does; other formats are decoded by your browser.
      • The silence before the sound is removed, the sound is normalised to 95 %, cut to the slot (36 608 bytes: 1.66 s at 22 kHz, 3.3 s at 11 kHz) with a very short fade at the end, and stored as 8-bit mu-law, like KAWA's own breaks. The waveform and Play show exactly that result.
      • Name: up to 11 characters (A-Z, 0-9, space and _ - . ! + #). It is what the FM-1 shows.
      • On the FM-1: a track's SOUND page (EDIT), engine SAMPLE, SOURCE = MY 1 to MY 4.
      • The samples live in the FM-1's flash: they stay when it is switched off, and are not part of a project file.
      • Without a browser: python3 install/kawa_samples.py put 1 kick.wav does the same.
